What’s the secret to lasting order?

Simplify Living is guided by the KonMari method, which serves as our framework. Of course, personal and individual aspects are always taken into account to create authentic and sustainable solutions.

The KonMari Method is a tidying approach developed by Marie Kondo to help you create your ideal, personalized living space. It guides you to thoroughly and completely organize your home. At the heart of the method is what’s called ‘Joy Checking’: you keep only what you truly need or what brings you joy, and let go of the rest. This creates more space for what really matters to you, while releasing anything that brings unpleasant feelings. Sounds good, doesn’t it

Marie Kondo

The process is simple and structured. We start with a free, no-obligation consultation to review your current situation, your goals, and any other important factors. Next, you sort your belongings category by category, keeping only what truly sparks joy. This ongoing process helps you gain clarity about what you want — and don’t want — in your home and life. In the end, we establish an organizational system that fits perfectly with what you choose to keep.

A home that is organized and aligned with your personal needs has an impact far beyond its four walls. Choosing the KonMari Method and fully tidying your space is a journey toward yourself. Tidying up isn’t just about letting go of the unnecessary — it’s also a process of pausing, reflecting, and making conscious decisions: What should stay? What truly matters to me?


With each step toward greater clarity in your surroundings, clarity also grows within. Many people find that after tidying, they move through life with a newfound lightness, suddenly knowing what truly nourishes them and what no longer fits. This newfound order creates not only space, but also time, energy, and mental clarity. It’s as if letting go of what’s unnecessary in your environment makes room for what truly matters to you.
